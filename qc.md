# Paper Airplanes — Quality check

Before a plane is shown as a preview:
- [ ] `node tools/check_planes.js` passes.
- [ ] The step pictures end in something that looks like the plane.
- [ ] Labels read in the right order and sit next to their line; arrows point the way the paper moves.
- [ ] `origin` and `blurb` are true and attributed.

Before a plane is ✅ approved (Claude, from real notes):
- [ ] Printed at 100% (the 2-inch bar measures 2 inches) and the back lined up (alignment test reads about 0, 0).
- [ ] Folded from the sheet alone, with no confusing step.
- [ ] 3 or more good flights logged (straight, or the loop it's meant to do).
